Env Info Banner

The banner reads <meta name="if-env-info"> on each page and shows what it finds. Pages without the tag are left alone.

Where it runs

Turn this off to silence the extension everywhere without uninstalling it.

One per line. * matches any characters, so *.test covers shop.test and api.shop.test.

How it looks

Off means the banner floats over the page instead. Sites with their own pinned header sometimes look better that way.
